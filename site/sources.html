Indoor air and health

  • 90% of time indoors; some pollutants 2 to 5 times higher indoors. U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, Report on the Environment: Indoor Air Quality.
  • 47% of U.S. homes with dampness or mold; 21% of current asthma (4.6 million cases); $3.5 billion a year. Mudarri and Fisk, Indoor Air, 2007, summarized by Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory.
  • 10 to 50% of indoor environments affected by dampness; health effects; recommendations; mycotoxins. Summary of the 2009 WHO Guidelines for Indoor Air Quality: Dampness and Mould, hosted by the CDC.
  • Formaldehyde sources and health effects. U.S. EPA, Formaldehyde and Indoor Air Quality.
  • Nearly 1 in 4 people genetically susceptible. A clinical estimate attributed to Dr. Ritchie Shoemaker's HLA-DR research, as described by MEpedia. This is one researcher's estimate and is not an established consensus figure.

Hemp-lime performance and code

  • ASTM E84: flame spread 0, smoke developed 0. Specimens prepared by Hempitecture, 2020, reported by HempToday.
  • ASTM E119: one-hour rating, 12-inch wall. Americhanvre, tested at Intertek, July 2024, reported by HempBuild Magazine.
  • 2024 IRC Appendix BL. Reported by HempBuild Magazine.
  • R-value, density, moisture, mold resistance, strength, and limits. Jellen and Memari, State-of-the-Art Review of Hempcrete for Residential Building Construction, Designs, 2025.
  • Carbon storage and weight. Manufacturer estimate (IsoHemp), as reported by Good Good Good.
