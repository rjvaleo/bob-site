The materials

What is hempcrete?

The woody core of the hemp plant mixed with a lime binder. It is cast, sprayed, or laid as block around a structural frame.

Is it structural?

No. Hempcrete is infill. The frame carries the house.

Will it burn?

Hempcrete scored 0 for flame spread and 0 for smoke developed under ASTM E84, and a 12-inch wall earned a one-hour rating under ASTM E119. See the test results

Will it grow mold?

Hempcrete is regarded as resistant to mold and insects because the lime is alkaline. Like any wall, it has to be detailed to stay dry. Long-term saturation can break down the hemp.

Is it allowed by code?

Yes. It is Appendix BL of the 2024 International Residential Code. Local jurisdictions decide whether to adopt the appendix.
