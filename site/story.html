Our story

Nobody made what our doctors told us to get. So we do.

A family made sick by its own home, and what we built because of it.

Our family was poisoned by mycotoxins in our own home. Every doctor said the same thing: you have to have a clean environment. That environment was impossible to get.

You have to take out your carpets. You have to leave behind what was in the old house. And then you find out that a house without mold is almost impossible to buy. So you have to build one.

We found the right doctors and we got well. Now we supply the materials other families need when they hear the same words we did.
