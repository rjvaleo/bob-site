Why hemp

A wall that does not burn, rot, or off-gas.

Hempcrete is the woody core of the hemp plant mixed with a lime binder. It has been tested in U.S. labs and written into the U.S. residential code.

The frame carries the house. The hemp does everything else.

  1. Frame. A structural frame carries the loads. Hempcrete is infill, not structure.
  2. Fill. Hempcrete is cast, sprayed, or laid as block around the frame, typically about 12 inches thick.
  3. Cure. The lime sets and the wall dries. It needs to be protected from rain while it does.
  4. Finish. A vapor-open lime or clay plaster goes straight on the wall. No drywall, no paint film, no vinyl.
Details for builders

Five layers of a conventional wall, in one.

  • Cavity insulation
  • Exterior sheathing
  • House wrap and vapor barrier
  • Interior drywall
  • Paint film

What to know before you build

  • Hempcrete is not load-bearing. Its compressive strength is a small fraction of concrete's.
  • It must be detailed to stay dry. Long-term saturation can break down the hemp, and freeze-thaw cycling weakens a wet wall.
  • Appendix BL is optional. Your state or local jurisdiction has to adopt it, or your building official has to accept it for your project.
  • A film-forming paint limits the wall's ability to manage moisture. Use a vapor-open finish.

Source: Jellen and Memari, Designs, 2025
