The code path

  • Hemp-lime is Appendix BL of the 2024 International Residential Code.
  • It is prescriptive for one- and two-family dwellings up to two stories in low seismic risk regions. No engineering required.
  • Taller buildings and higher seismic regions need an engineered design.
  • Appendices are optional. If your jurisdiction has not adopted BL, it can be proposed to the building official for your project.

Source: HempBuild Magazine
