Programs open nationwide

USDA Section 504 Home Repair

Who qualifies: Owner-occupants in eligible rural areas with low or very low income. Grants require age 62 or older.

What it covers: Loans for repairs and improvements. Grants for removing health and safety hazards.

Amount: Grants up to $10,000. Loans up to $40,000 at 1% fixed for 20 years. Up to $50,000 combined.

Start with usUSDA Rural Development

DOE Weatherization Assistance Program

Who qualifies: Low-income households. Apply through your state or one of more than 700 local providers.

What it covers: Energy upgrades, with health and safety measures that include mold and moisture evaluation and ventilation.

Amount: No cost to the household. About 35,000 homes are served each year.

Start with usU.S. Department of Energy

HUD Healthy Homes Production

Who qualifies: Households served by a funded agency. HUD awards the money to state and local agencies and nonprofits, which run the local programs.

What it covers: Fixing several home health hazards in one project.

Amount: More than $93 million awarded to 52 agencies in January 2025. Local limits vary.

Start with usU.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development

FHA 203(k) Rehabilitation Loan

Who qualifies: Buyers and owners who meet FHA mortgage requirements.

What it covers: Repairs rolled into a purchase or refinance mortgage.

Amount: Limited version covers up to $75,000 in repairs.

Georgia Community HOME Investment Program

Who qualifies: Low-income homeowners, through local governments, nonprofits, and housing authorities funded by the Georgia Department of Community Affairs.

What it covers: Housing rehabilitation.

Amount: Set by the local program.

Start with usProgram summary

DeKalb County Special Purpose Home Repair

Who qualifies: Seniors 62 and older and disabled adults at or below 60% of area median income.

What it covers: Health and safety repairs.

Amount: Forgivable loans up to $14,000.

Start with usProgram summary

Cherokee County Home Repair Program

Who qualifies: Homeowners 62 and older at or below 60% of area median income.

What it covers: Roofs, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, windows and doors.

Amount: Grants up to $15,000.

Start with usProgram summary

Savannah Home Repair Assistance

Who qualifies: Income-qualified Savannah homeowners.

What it covers: Repairs that make the home safe and livable.

Amount: Grants up to $2,000 a year. Loans up to $15,000.
